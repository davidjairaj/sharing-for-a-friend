CODEX SERAPHINIANUS

by Suraaj Chandra Jha

In 1981 Luigi Serafini published a 359 page encyclopedia of a world that does not exist, captioned throughout in a script nobody has ever read. Forty five years of decoding attempts, and one more announced in June 2026. A cryptanalysis, and the translation that survives it.

RESEARCH NOTE

A CRYPTANALYSIS, AND THE TRANSLATION THAT SURVIVES IT

The script is asemic: the shape of writing with none of the content. The page numbers are base 21 and decode cleanly. The pictures say a great deal. Read the short version on the web, or take the full note with the evidence, the audit and the sources. It opens full screen. You press next.

the author

Suraaj Chandra Jha is a hacker in the old, joyful sense of the word: interested in systems, evidence, and the small anomaly that gives the whole game away. The books explain machines without ceremony. The notes begin where an odd system or an unearned claim refuses to go away.
