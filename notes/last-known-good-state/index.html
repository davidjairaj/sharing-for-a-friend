THE LAST KNOWN GOOD STATE

How to resume work without inventing its history.

by Suraaj Chandra Jha
Free to read here, free to download, free to pass on.

the finding

The past is not the thing you need.

A transcript can preserve every sentence and leave the next operator stranded. A snapshot can preserve every file and omit the reason those files are safe. A summary can sound decisive while quietly mixing observation, intention and guesswork.

A useful recovery record is smaller and stricter: a checked snapshot with an exact identity, a causal frontier, live unresolved edges, governing intent, provenance and the first safe move.

the title is a test

Each word has to earn its place.

Remove one word and the phrase becomes comfort rather than recovery. Add one word, resume, and the record must also carry intent, unresolved effects, authority and a checked next action.

LastAn exact coordinate in history, not “recent”.
KnownEvidence, provenance and confidence attached.
GoodAn explicit invariant actually observed.
StateThe dependencies needed to continue.
ResumeLive edges, intent and a safe entry point.
bad memories

More history is not more continuity.

The incoming operator needs every fact that can change the next safe action. Everything else is evidence to consult, not an interface to replay.

the archive

Remembers too much.

Old failures, abandoned ideas and later corrections coexist without a current address. Rich evidence becomes an expensive search problem.

the snapshot

Remembers too little.

A directory tree says what bytes exist. It does not say which ones passed, what remains outside the tree, or why a constraint must survive.

the summary

Can lie without lying.

“Deployment pending” may mean requested, acknowledged or applied. Sincere prose fails when observation and intention arrive as the same kind of sentence.

machines that return

Recovery engineering already knows the shape.

Different systems preserve different objects, but their failures converge: identity, dependent state, history position and uncertain effects cannot be reconstructed from a plausible pile of files.

GitA tree plus a route through history.A commit joins one directory state to parents, people, time and message. Exact identity is necessary, not sufficient.
SQLite WALThe live edge may be another file.In write-ahead logging mode, separating a database from its WAL can discard committed work or damage the database.
Distributed snapshotsProcesses and channels form one state.Several locally accurate captures can compose an impossible scene when messages in flight cross the cut.
CRIU and DMTCPFiles are only the visible organs.Useful checkpoints reconstruct memory, descriptors, pipes, sockets, signals, credentials and process relationships.
Raft and etcdA snapshot needs history coordinates.Log index, term, cluster identity and revision determine what can safely follow a restored state.
Client retriesSilence proves nothing.A service may commit and crash before replying. Durable request identity separates a safe retry from a duplicate effect.
taking over

The receiver closes the circuit.

High-consequence handovers do not treat a completed form as a transfer. They make the receiver inspect the world, question the model and assume responsibility explicitly.

Air traffic control

The FAA sequence is preview, brief, assume, review. The incoming controller reads live displays first, then receives exceptions, asks questions and takes control explicitly.

Incident command

FEMA places situation status beside objectives, priorities, organisation, resources, facilities, communications and concerns. Facts alone do not carry purpose or authority.

Industrial shifts

The UK Health and Safety Executive recommends outgoing preparation, two-way exchange and incoming cross-check. Written and verbal forms solve different parts of the transfer.

Interrupted tasks

Altmann and Trafton found that keeping a task display visible helped resumption under some longer interruption conditions. Using a written goal or next action is an inference from that bounded result, not something their study tested.

Inspect. Question. Test. Assume.
the interrupted spool

A theory of recovery should survive an interruption.

Beacon Spool is a clean-room, dependency-free Python queue made for this note. It claims JSON jobs, writes deterministic receipts, quarantines malformed or conflicting inputs byte for byte, and restarts after injected process exits.

The frozen worktree

A base commit passes twelve legacy tests. Interrupted recovery work adds four more. Fourteen pass. Two fail around claimed-job recovery and an existing conflicting receipt. The files contain no helpful TODO pointing at the answer.

14 / 16visible tests at the interruption point

The exact same worktree and task go to every operator. Only the continuity packet changes.

No operator runs are reported here. The protocol does not smuggle in a conclusion. A scored study must freeze the contract and verifier, withhold sealed scenarios from operators, then release them with the results. The reference evaluator scores legacy compatibility, crash recovery, duplicate safety, byte-preserving quarantine, determinism and filesystem boundaries. A high total cannot hide a failed safety group. No result from one synthetic spool can establish a universal minimum. The evaluator executes candidate Python; its temporary roots isolate tests, not hostile code.

the recovery cut

Eight fields. One restartable claim.

This is a proposed sufficient capsule. Its usefulness is operational: each field rules out a distinct way of resuming the wrong work, at the wrong point, with inherited confidence.

01

Identity and authority

Exact work, anchor, scope, owner.

02

Intent

Objective, success, constraints, decisions.

03

Material snapshot

Artefacts, data, environment, relationships.

04

Causal frontier

Committed, applied, attempted, observed.

05

Open edges

In-flight effects, blockers, risks, unknowns.

06

Provenance

Sources, actors, time, assumptions, confidence.

07

Restart cue

Prerequisites and exact first useful action.

08

Verification

Invariants to rerun and receiver cross-check.

Remove a field. Watch a failure appear.

No identityResume the wrong instance.
No intentContinue correctly toward the wrong result.
No snapshotPossess a story with nothing runnable.
No frontierRepeat or omit completed work.
No open edgesLose or duplicate live effects.
No provenanceFill gaps with inherited confidence.
No restart cueOwn the state but cannot enter it.
No verificationMistake a plausible state for a good one.
